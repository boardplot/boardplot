What it means

  1. Price increases added 1.6 points, the only positive driver.

What it means

  1. Apparel lost 2.5 points and is the largest category at €14.9m.
  2. Footwear lost 2.0 points: freight and duties hit imported shoes hardest.
  3. Bike packs launched this year at a 44% margin and dilute the mix.
Exhibit 3

What it means

  1. Tents, backpacks and footwear raised prices and still sold more units.
  2. Apparel cut prices by 1.5% and still sold 3.9% fewer units.
  3. Accessories bought volume with lower prices at the highest margin of all categories.
