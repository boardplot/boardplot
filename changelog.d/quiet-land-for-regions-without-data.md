### Changed / may need action

- Choropleth regions and tile map tiles that the data does not list now draw as pale land, and the "Not in data" key swatch draws in the same paint. Before, each of these regions had a page-colour fill and the dark region outline, so a partial world map of 38 countries drew its darkest lines around the 200 countries without data. The land is the theme's light neutral washed toward the page (`#E7EBEE` in `standard`). It is chosen again in each theme the chart is painted in: it stays a small step from the page and moves closer to the page where it would read like a colour of the scale. A diverging map with a grey midpoint therefore gets a paler land. A choropleth region without data now has a thin edge in the page colour and draws before the regions with data, so their outlines stay whole. A tile without data has no outline: the gap between tiles parts it from its neighbours. The key swatch has no outline. Regions with `value: null` keep their hatch and their outline.
- A choropleth no longer prints automatic codes on regions the data does not list. This applies to every pack except `world`, which already labelled only regions with data. Before, an EU map with six listed countries also printed codes, and long leaders, on the 21 countries without data. Tile maps keep a code on every tile.
- Old look: the region or tile fill was `{ token: 'background' }` with a `{ token: 'inkMuted' }` stroke at the `hair` width (0.75 px), and the key swatch was an outlined, hollow square. No theme key or option brings back that land. To print the codes on regions without data again, set `labels: 'all'` on the choropleth, or name the regions in `labelRegions`.
