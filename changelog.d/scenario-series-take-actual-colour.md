### Changed / may need action

- Column: a series with `scenario: "PY"`, `"PL"` or `"FC"` now takes the colour of the actual it is compared with, in every theme. The k-th PY, PL or FC series pairs with the k-th actual series (`scenario: "AC"` or no scenario), in authored order, and takes no colour step of its own. A series whose cells all carry one scenario counts as that scenario. PY draws the actual's colour as the 45 % wash toward the page, FC hatches it and PL outlines it. Series order no longer changes the colours. Where the page floor leaves no wash apart from the actual (an actual near the floor on a dark page), PY steps toward the ink instead, so it never prints as the actual. On a dark page an FC hatch beside its actual lifts its stripes to 4.5:1 against the page, so a forecast stays visible next to the solid actual. A PY cell in a series of several scenarios draws the same wash. A stack's segments keep their stack colours, and a series with its own `fill` or `colorRole` keeps it.
- Before, such a series kept the colour of its place in the series list, and a PY cell beside other series drew the full series colour. With PY, AC, PL and FC in that order, PY took the theme's first series colour, AC the second, PL the AC colour and FC the fourth. In `notation` the prior year then printed darker than the actual. To bring back the old look, give each series its old colour as `fill`. For that order: `boardroom` PY `#12395E`, AC and PL `#5E99C5`, FC `#72767C`; `standard` `#075A8E`, `#55BEF7`, `#838CDA`; `standard-dark` `#087397`, `#55BEFB`, `#828BD9`; `notation` `#6D6D6D`, `#2A2A2A`, `#4A4A4A`; `notation-dark` `#606060`, `#858585`, `#D5D5D5`; `capital` `#2E346F`, `#C48404`, `#905111`; `studio` `#24564E`, `#C68610`, `#609E93`; `glass` `#4E8BE6`, `#35CDB3`, `#B5F5C8`. For another order, the n-th series took the theme's n-th colour (`theme.color.series`).
