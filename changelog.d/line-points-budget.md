### Fixed

- A line or area chart with very many points or very many series no longer runs the CLI or the MCP server out of memory. A line of 1,999 categories by 200 series (about 400,000 points) used all of a 4 GB heap and aborted the process after about 14 seconds, and 100 categories by 2,000 series took 11 seconds and 2 GB. A line or area now refuses with `too-many-items` at `/data/series` when its cost is over 300,000, where the cost is the drawn categories times the series plus 100 for each series. The fix names fewer series, aggregated periods, `window.last` or `viewDomain`. The count is taken after the viewport and after the sampling of a dated line. A line of 1,000 categories by 201 series, and one of 1,000 by 100, are unchanged. A combo chart refuses the same way when its cost is over 20,000, where the cost is the drawn categories times all its series plus 40 for each series, because it labels and stacks every point: 1,000 categories by 101 series ran out of memory after about 80 seconds, 10 by 1,000 did not finish in 150 seconds, and 300 by 51 is unchanged. A column or bar chart with many series keeps its limit on categories only.
