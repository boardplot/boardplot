### Fixed

- A line or area chart with very many points no longer runs the CLI or the MCP server out of memory. A line of 1,999 categories by 200 series (about 400,000 points) used all of a 4 GB heap and aborted the process after about 14 seconds. A line or area now refuses with `too-many-items` at `/data/series` when it draws more than 200,000 points, and the fix names fewer series, aggregated periods, `window.last` or `viewDomain`. The count is taken after the viewport and after the sampling of a dated line. A chart above the limit that used to finish near the memory edge now refuses too, such as a line of 1,000 categories by 201 series. A line of 1,000 categories by 100 series is unchanged. A combo chart refuses the same way above 15,000 points (drawn categories times all its series), because it labels and stacks every point: 1,000 categories by 101 series ran out of memory after about 80 seconds, and 300 by 50 took up to 21 seconds.
