### Fixed

- A table whose `options.unit` names a scale ("$k", "EUR m", "M units") no longer adds its own magnitude on top of it. The unit states the scale of the data as authored, as on the bar table, the combo axis and the other charts. Before, values of a million or more without a format climbed to M or B: under "$k", 8,540,000 printed as "8.5" with the subtitle "Revenue in M", and the hover card and accessible text read "$8.5 k M". Small values under such a unit printed as "0.0" (12,345 under "M units"). The table now prints the whole figure, grouped: "8,540,000" under "in $k", "12,345" and "−12,345" under "M units". To scale the figures and print the letter as before, give the column a format such as `"format": "1.2M"`.
