### Changed / may need action

- Heatmap matrices stagger long column names on two tiers before they rotate. When names would turn 45 degrees, they now alternate between an upper and a lower tier, each name up to two columns wide on one or two lines, joined to its column by a hairline tick. A name turns only when two tiers cannot hold every name whole. A row of names that already stands flat on three lines keeps those lines, and a month row keeps its own thinning and initials. The staggered header takes about half the height of the turned one, so a matrix with long names fits a smaller height, and a bubble matrix with long names needs a lower minimum height. The two tiers also sit 3 px apart instead of 2 px, so a name at the top of the plot is never hidden by the label spacing.
  Old look: every column name turned 45 degrees and was cut with an ellipsis once its text passed the header room, and the tiers of a staggered bubble matrix sat 2 px apart. No option brings it back, because a name that can stand upright is never turned on purpose.
