### Fixed

- A PNG line with a character that the theme font lacks, such as `→` in a subtitle, now keeps its own face when the line has text at weight 500 or 600. Before, the PNG drew the whole line bold in a condensed fallback face. The Bridge design asks for 500 and 600, and the PNG renderer accepted only a font face with exactly that weight, while Helvetica Neue and Arial have 400 and 700. The renderer now picks the face by the CSS weight rules, as browsers do. This applies to every text role: title, subtitle, notes, source, labels and table headers. The SVG output does not change. The PNGs of these goldens change: `pvm/fy-revenue-bridge.authored`, `pvm/fy-revenue-combined-volume-mix.authored`, `pvm/mix-dominates-sign-flip.authored`, `pvm/revenue-bridge-no-net-change.authored`, `pvm/segment-pvm-interaction-split.authored`, `table/receipts-changelog.authored` and `waterfall/cost-bridge-lower-is-better.authored`.
