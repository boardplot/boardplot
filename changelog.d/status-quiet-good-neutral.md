### Changed / may need action

- A status chart now draws its good and neutral cells washed toward the page, so off-track and at-risk cells carry the colour. A good cell takes `color.goodFill` washed up to 78 % (on the default light theme a red-amber-green board draws `#BCE4D3` instead of `#0FA063`). A neutral cell, such as "Not started" or "Hold", takes `color.neutralMark` washed up to 67 % (`#CBD0D5` instead of `#5E6C7B`), so it no longer outweighs "Off track". Beside a neutral state the good cell stays a deeper green (`#7DCCAB`) to keep apart from the grey. Each wash steps back toward its full colour until the cell stays apart from a cell with no status and from every other state colour, also for red-green colour vision. It is decided again in the theme the chart is painted in, so a dark theme washes toward its dark page, stepped back until the cell reads against the dark page as clearly as the light wash does on white. A hatched good or neutral state keeps its full colour under its hatch, and a plain state of the same tone then stays a step stronger than that hatch ground, so an actual never reads lighter than its forecast. Other charts that paint `color.goodFill` or `color.neutralMark` do not change, and `colorblindSafe` keeps its good and bad colours. A theme patch of `color.goodFill` or `color.neutralMark` does not bring back the old look, because the wash applies to any colour. To bring back the old look, set the theme override `{ "marks": { "statusFill": "full" } }`.

### Added

- Theme field `marks.statusFill`: `'quiet'` (default) or `'full'`. `'full'` fills a status chart's good and neutral cells with `color.goodFill` and `color.neutralMark` as they are.
