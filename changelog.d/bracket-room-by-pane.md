### Changed / may need action

- Butterfly and pyramid charts give the bars back the room that a comparison bracket used to take from both panes. Under the shared scale the two panes now differ in width: the pane that holds a bracket takes the bracket's room from its own side and the name column moves off centre, while both panes keep the same scale. A comparison label that goes to the note list no longer keeps any room, so the bars use the full pane. Before, a half-slide butterfly with a difference bracket drew its longest bar at 20 px in a 576 px chart, and a pyramid whose comparison label moved to the note list drew the same stubs beside a blank half. The affected fixtures are `butterfly/period-comparisons`, `pyramid/dated-population-comparison` and `pyramid/dated-share-difference`. Bar lengths on a shared scale stay in proportion, so no value changes. No option brings back the old equal panes: they were the fault. Charts without a comparison bracket draw as before.
