### Added

- The diagram chart has a new variant, `architecture`, for system architecture and data flow exhibits. Zones sit side by side, or `options.flow: 'lanes'` stacks labelled layers on a shade ramp. A node can name a `track`, which lines it up across zones or lanes, and `span: 'full'`, which draws a wide band or a rail. An edge can set `flow: 'return'`, which keeps it out of the node order and on the outer corridors. `data.channels` gives several edges one shared label in the gutter they cross. Edges with a common end share a trunk with a junction dot, and unrelated edges keep 8 px apart. Zones wrap into bands and nodes into fewer columns as the width falls, down to one column at 320 px, and every name stays complete. A layout that cannot keep lines off text is refused with `diagram-route-unresolved`, a forward cycle with `diagram-cycle`, and two nodes in one cell with `diagram-track-conflict`.
