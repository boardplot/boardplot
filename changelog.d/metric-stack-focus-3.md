### Added

- Under `stack.variant: 'focus'` only the focus categories print their values, bold on a plate in the page colour. A category needs room for its mark only, 14 px, plus its name where names print, so about 30 weekly columns fit beside the readout at 1,184 px. With `data.periods` the period row is the axis: each period label starts at its first category after a short tick, and no category prints its name.
