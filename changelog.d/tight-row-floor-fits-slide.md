### Changed / may need action

- A status board and a population pyramid now draw in a pinned frame that was refused before. A status board no longer holds every row to a fixed 28 px: a row needs its name's height plus the label gap, about 24 px at slide size, so a 12-row board draws on a full slide (888 x 396 pt). A board that cuts a row name keeps the 28 px floor, so a half slide still refuses a 12-row board with long names. On a pinned height, a pyramid charges each row the ink height of its text plus 4 px, instead of a full line box, and a row tighter than a line of text takes one type step down to the target's readability floor (12 pt on a slide) for its names and values, so a 21-band pyramid draws every label on a full slide. An unpinned status board or pyramid keeps its natural height. The old look is a frame at the former minimum height: 417 pt for a 12-row board on a full slide (the refusal names the height), and 510.75 pt for a 21-band pyramid on a full slide. A frame that drew before draws the same, except a pinned pyramid whose rows were tighter than a line of text, whose type now steps down once.
