### Fixed

- A grid of tiers on one category axis now keeps the height its parts need. Rotated category names no longer run off the bottom of the canvas over the source line, because the grid counts the box of a rotated name when it packs the tiers. A tier that the weights gave less than its own minimum height no longer collapses to a flat strip when the canvas holds every minimum. On a short canvas, a tier that keeps its frame at the plot floor no longer pushes the stack up until its caption prints over the subtitle: the tiers with plot to spare give up height, down to their minimum. When the tiers still do not fit, the grid warns with `grid-panel-too-short`. A tier grid placed in rows, with no authored height, draws at the height its panels ask for: it no longer ends a few pixels short, squashing the base panel and moving value labels to a note.
