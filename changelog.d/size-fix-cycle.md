### Fixed

- Following the size fixes of a warning no longer loops or turns a drawn chart into a refusal. The `plot-under-floor`, `aspect-cap`, `height-off-ladder` and `plot-under-min` fixes were each computed from their own rule, so a column at 480 px wide went from 463 to 361 to 484 and back to 361, a diagram went from a drawn 416 to a refused 241, and a heatmap went from a refusal to a draw at 414 and then to a refused 518. A render that draws now reports these four warnings with no `fix`. The warning and its message stay, and `suggest` says to call `measure` for a verified size, to widen the chart, or to leave the height out. A refusal keeps its `fix`. Render draws no extra layouts for this.
- `boardplot_measure` and the `measure` command draw every height they name. For `aspect-cap`, `height-off-ladder` and `plot-under-min` they report the warning with a `fix` that names the first height of the standard ladder that draws with no size warning, nearest first and within the aspect cap, or with no `fix` when none does. A `plot-under-floor` height is drawn through the full render path before it is reported.
