### Fixed

- Following the size fixes of a warning no longer loops or turns a drawn chart into a refusal. The `plot-under-floor`, `aspect-cap`, `height-off-ladder` and `plot-under-min` fixes were each computed from their own rule, so a column at 480 px wide went from 463 to 361 to 484 and back to 361, a diagram went from a drawn 416 to a refused 241, and a heatmap went from a refusal to a draw at 414 and then to a refused 518. A render that draws now reports these four warnings with no `fix`. The warning and its message stay, and `suggest` says to call `measure` for a verified size, to widen the chart, or to leave the height out. A refusal keeps its `fix`. Render draws no extra layouts for this. The layout that React reports through `onWarnings` follows the same rule: its `plot-under-floor` warning gives an estimated height in the message and no `fix`.
- `boardplot_measure` and the `measure` command draw every height they name. For `aspect-cap`, `height-off-ladder` and `plot-under-min` they report the warning with a `fix` that names the first height of the standard ladder that draws with no size warning, nearest first and within the aspect cap, or with no `fix` when none does. A `plot-under-floor` height is drawn through the full render path before it is reported.
- A diagram refusal that comes from a `--width` or `--height` argument now names that argument in its `fix`, as the other size refusals do. Before, its `fix` patched `/options/size`, which the argument overrides.
- The wider-size `fix` of `table-overflow` on a table with a fixed height now names the height the wider frame needs. It kept the authored height before, and a table that had drawn at 1048 by 379 px was refused at the 1134 by 379 px that the `fix` named.
