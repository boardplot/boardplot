### Changed / may need action

- A histogram whose counts no longer fit their bins now prints only the tallest bin's count, and the frequency axis carries the scale. This applies when more than 12 bins hold observations and a count wider than its bin loses its seat over the bar. Before, every count was still seated: counts lifted onto leaders, empty bins in a long tail printed rows of 0s, a note listed the counts without a seat, and up to three tail callouts named bins under a pixel. A 117-bin chart of a long-tailed measure now shows its bars, the axis and one count. A 34-bin chart whose one count was lifted onto a leader now shows the axis and its peak count. The engine warns `histogram-counts-peak-only` (info). A chart with 12 or fewer filled bins, a hidden frequency axis (`valueAxis.show: false`) or `labels.point: false` is unchanged. To bring back the old look, set `labelDensity: "full"`.
