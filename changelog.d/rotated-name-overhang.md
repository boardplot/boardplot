### Fixed

- A narrow grid of column panels with long category names no longer throws "bandScale range must contain finite ascending values" in `measure()`, and no longer draws 2 px columns. A rotated first name took room from the left of the plot, and the row was then planned again in the narrower plot. There it often stopped rotating and drew no name, so the room stood for nothing; in a narrow probe it was wider than the plot and the band scale threw. The plot now gives that room only where the narrower row still rotates and draws the name. Elsewhere the plot keeps its width and the names print upright, cut with an ellipsis and listed in the notes. Column, combo, variance and waterfall share this rule. The room a rotated name takes also leaves every plot at least 10 px, line included.
