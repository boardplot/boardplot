### Fixed

- A step note on a waterfall with `valueAxis.break: "auto"` keeps its leader. The break leaves little room over the top bar, so both notes stacked at a free spot with a short stub or no leader and did not show which step each belonged to. The band over the plot now grows until the notes take the seats that lead back to their steps, as on the same chart without a break. A chart whose notes already have a seat is unchanged.
