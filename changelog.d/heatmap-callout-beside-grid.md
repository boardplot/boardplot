### Changed

- A heatmap callout that has a seat beside the grid at one width keeps that seat at every wider width. The seat was sized from the callout text in bold, but the callout prints in the regular weight and can break its lines at another word, so at a few widths its text was wider than the seat. The callout then left its row: on a 580 px report it stood beside another row, two rows above its cell, without a leader, on a 600 px report it went under the grid with the column name in front of its text, and with `legend: 'right'` at 720 px it was left out. With a set height of 405 px the same chart was refused at 580 and 600 px although it drew at 560 and 620 px. The callout now stays beside its cell, and the plot gives up the few pixels its text needs. No theme or option brings back the old seat. `preferSide: 'below'` on the callout puts it under the grid on purpose.
