### Changed / may need action

- A standalone KPI card in a slot of fixed height fills the slot. With a given height (`--height`, `options.size` with a height, a size preset), the figure grows to a quarter of the slot height and the delta to 30% of the figure, never under their natural sizes. The note grows to half the delta size. The delta grows only while it fits the card width on one or two lines, and the figure steps down until the whole block fits the free height, so no text that the natural sizes draw is hidden. The value, note and delta then sit in the middle of the free height between the title and the source. In an 888 × 396 pt slide slot the figure prints at 132 px instead of 40 px. This applies to the delta, value-only and note forms. A card without a fixed height keeps its natural size, and grid panels and cards with a spark, compare chips or an attainment keep their layout. The old look printed the natural sizes at the top of the slot. To keep it, set the new theme field `marks.kpiSlotFill` to `false`: `{ "theme": { "preset": "standard", "overrides": { "marks": { "kpiSlotFill": false } } } }`. The fixture `kpi/slot-natural-sizes` shows it.
