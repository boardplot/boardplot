### Changed / may need action

- A cascade bar table now keys every colour it draws. A table with row groups lists the grey rest rows in its key as "Rest rows". A table with two or more segments or a range lists them too when it has rest rows. The Total bar beside a key of group colours no longer repeats one of them. Before, a theme whose first series colour is its `total` colour drew the Total bar in the colour of that group. Now the Total deepens toward black on a light page and toward white on a dark page until it stands apart from every key colour, and it decides again in each theme. A theme whose `total` colour already stands apart draws the Total as before, and a cascade without row groups keeps the `total` colour. The old colour is the theme's `total` colour, for example `#12395E` in the boardroom theme. To bring back the old look, set `cascade: { "totalApart": false }`: the Total bar then keeps the theme's `total` colour as it is, even where it equals a group colour. A theme patch on `color.total` cannot bring it back, because the Total is deepened again whenever it reads as a group colour.
- A compact cascade bar table with several periods names each period at its bars. Before, its key showed one swatch per period that differed by a 2 px stripe, because every bar was one colour. Now the first row prints each period right of the end of its track, and the key swatches are gone. The tracks end a little earlier, to leave room for the longest name. The key of a compact table without cascade keeps its swatches.
- The width floor of a compact cascade bar table with several periods now includes the room for the longest period name, capped at a third of the plot. `sizing.minWidth` of such a table rises, most where the periods differ in size on the shared scale or a name is long, so the shortest track keeps its floor at that width. Before, a table could be drawn at its stated minimum with tracks shorter than the floor.
