### Changed / may need action

- A cascade bar table now keys every colour it draws. A table with row groups lists the grey rest rows in its key as "Rest rows". A table with two or more segments or a range lists them too when it has rest rows. The Total bar beside a key of group colours no longer repeats one of them. Before, a theme whose first series colour is its `total` colour drew the Total bar in the colour of that group. Now the Total deepens toward black on a light page and toward white on a dark page until it stands apart from every key colour, and it decides again in each theme. A theme whose `total` colour already stands apart draws the Total as before, and a cascade without row groups keeps the `total` colour. The old colour is the theme's `total` colour, for example `#12395E` in the boardroom theme. No option brings it back, because it would make the Total read as a group again.
- A compact cascade bar table with several periods names each period at its bars. Before, its key showed one swatch per period that differed by a 2 px stripe, because every bar was one colour. Now the first row prints each period right of the end of its track, and the key swatches are gone. The tracks end a little earlier, to leave room for the longest name. The key of a compact table without cascade keeps its swatches.
