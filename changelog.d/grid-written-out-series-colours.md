### Fixed

- A grid written out in `data.panels` now colours a series alike in every panel where panels share series ids. Before, each panel coloured its series by position, so a series listed second in one panel and first in the next changed colour, and the key listed it twice with a `series-indistinguishable` warning. Panels that list the same ids in the same order, or that share no id, keep their colours. A series with its own `fill`, `colorRole` or `scenario` keeps it. A panel that sets `highlight`, `emphasis` or a `palette` (its own or the grid's), or that gives a series a `fill` or `colorRole`, keeps its own colouring and takes no part in the lock.
