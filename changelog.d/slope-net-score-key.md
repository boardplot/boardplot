### Changed / may need action

- A diverging slope chart (`variant: "diverging"`) now prints the net score under each panel as numbers only, such as `+20.9 (+7.9)`, and defines it once in the key row, after the period key: **Net** = Better − Worse in FY26 (change vs FY24). The name takes the emphasis weight. When the definition does not fit on the period key's row, it takes a row of its own, and it wraps on a narrow card. Before, each panel printed `Net +20.9 (+7.9)` (German `Netto +20,9 (+7,9)`). On a wide slide with eight panels that text was wider than a panel, so four or five net scores went to a note that an image does not show. The definition was a note outside the frame: `Net = Better − Worse in FY26; change vs FY24 in brackets.` With `diverging.net: false`, the key item goes away together with the net row. No option brings back the word in each panel or the definition as a note. To keep a definition below the chart as well, add it to `options.footnotes`, for example `"footnotes": ["Net = Better − Worse in FY26; change vs FY24 in brackets."]`.
