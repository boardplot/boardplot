### Fixed

- A column, bar, line (also with `valueAxis.percent`) or treemap chart that prints only shares now names the percent in its unit line. A 100% bar with the subtitle measure "Volume share" and `unit: "kt"` read "Volume share in kt" over percent labels. It now reads "Volume share in %". A treemap with `cellValue: "share"` and no key of group totals no longer prints the value unit as a subtitle. The unit stays when an absolute figure prints too, such as `grandTotalLabels: true`, absolute column segment values (`labels.segment` with `absolute`, or `shareLabels`), a treemap `total` or a key with group totals. An authored subtitle is never rewritten. An authored unit clause such as "Revenue in $m" stays as written and warns `share-unit-conflict` with a fix that drops the clause.
- A 100% column or bar with a `format` that has no % sign now warns `format-ignored`, because the format changes no share label.
- A mekko with `variant: "stacked"` or `"bar"` and a percent `unit` over data series that declare an amount unit now warns `percent-unit-over-amounts`, with the fix of setting `options.unit` to the amount unit.
- A stacked mekko of percent cells now keeps the percent sign on its segment labels, column totals and value axis, with the digits that keep small values apart: percent cells of 0.4, 0.9 and 1.2 print "0.4%", "0.9%" and "1.2%" on an axis of "0.0%", "0.5%" and "1.0%".
