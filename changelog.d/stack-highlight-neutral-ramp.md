### Changed / may need action

- A highlighted series in a stacked or 100% column, a stacked or 100% bar or a mekko now is the only colour on the chart. When two or more other series have no `fill` or `colorRole`, they take a neutral ramp in authored order: the first takes the muted mark (the grey a single receding series already takes), the last takes `color.neutralLight`, with even steps between. The ramp is decided in the active theme, so a repaint to dark decides again. Where the muted mark sits too close to `color.neutralLight` for the steps to read apart, as on a dark page, the ramp starts at the neutral mark. Before, a column stack and a mekko gave every other series its categorical colour, so the accent was one of several colours and could sit next to another blue. A stacked bar painted the other series in two alternating greys, so the first and third series looked the same. The `series-adjacent-similar` warning holds two neighbouring ramp steps to the ordered step floor (3 OKLab), as it does for the stack ramp. To bring back the old look, set `fill` on each plain series to the colour it drew before. In the default theme, a five-series column stack with its third series highlighted drew `#12395E`, `#5E99C5`, `#72767C` and `#6F69CC` for the other four. A four-series mekko with its first series highlighted drew `#5E99C5`, `#B58629` and `#72767C`. A stacked bar drew `#5E6C7B` and `#CFD9E2` in turn.
