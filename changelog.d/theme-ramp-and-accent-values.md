### Changed / may need action

- `boardroom`: the accent is a calmer blue. `color.highlight`, `color.status.won` and `color.status.gantt-special` change from `#1D4ED8` to `#10529E`, the accent `standard` uses. It stays apart from the total `#12395E`. To bring back the old look, patch the theme with `{ "color": { "highlight": "#1D4ED8", "status": { "won": "#1D4ED8", "gantt-special": "#1D4ED8" } } }`.
- `boardroom`: the table secondary ink is a neutral slate. `table.inks.secondary` changes from `#305B86` to `#5B6878`, so unjudged changes, units and a running period print in grey and only a judged change has a hue. To bring back the old look, patch the theme with `{ "table": { "inks": { "secondary": "#305B86" } } }`.
- Every preset now has a stack ramp (`color.stack`). A stacked or 100% column or bar in these presets paints its plain series from dark to light on one hue instead of the categorical colours: `notation` `#2A2A2A`, `#818181`, `#DADADA`; `notation-dark` `#D5D5D5`, `#858585`, `#4A4A4A`; `studio` `#24564E`, `#609E93`, `#C4DAD3`; `capital` `#2E346F`, `#818BC3`, `#C1C6DF`; `glass` `#C4D9F9`, `#4E8BE6`, `#2D4870`. A dark page runs strongest first, as `standard-dark` does. Before, a stack with more series than the categorical ramp repeated the last colour, so in `notation` the top segments of an eight-series column printed as one black block, and `studio` painted a segment in its red `#C1553F`. To bring back the old look, set `color.stack` to the theme's old `color.series`, one entry per stacked series. For an eight-series stack in `notation`: `{ "color": { "stack": ["#6D6D6D", "#2A2A2A", "#939393", "#4A4A4A", "#0C0C0C", "#0C0C0C", "#0C0C0C", "#0C0C0C"] } }`. The old series of `studio` are `#24564E`, `#C68610`, `#C1553F`, `#609E93`, `#7B82C7`, `#84538F`, of `capital` `#2E346F`, `#C48404`, `#1F7761`, `#905111`, `#8692A2`, and of `glass` `#4E8BE6`, `#35CDB3`, `#C6A4FF`, `#B5F5C8`, `#E9558F`, `#387008`, `#F0A018`, `#885080`. A `notation-dark` stack took its old series, listed below.
- The dark presets and `notation` put the strongest colour first. The first series is the one with the most contrast on the page, so a lead line or the actual is never the quietest mark. `standard-dark` swaps its first two series colours (and the same two in its `atlantic` palette). `notation` and `notation-dark` reorder their greys and keep every neighbour 25 OKLab or more apart. Fills that are tints of the first series (pie, heatmap ladder and Harvey balls, cohort, choropleth, tilemap and table heat ramps) follow the new first colour, so in `notation` they run to near black. In `notation-dark` a column's actual now prints white and its prior year grey. Before, the actual was the dimmest grey. To bring back the old look, patch the theme's `color.series`: `standard-dark` `["#087397", "#55BEFB", "#25AAA2", "#828BD9", "#1E9240", "#A054C4", "#F8C030", "#60E8A8"]` and `color.palettes.atlantic` `["#087397", "#55BEFB", "#25AAA2", "#828BD9", "#1E9240", "#A054C4"]`; `notation` `["#6D6D6D", "#2A2A2A", "#939393", "#4A4A4A", "#0C0C0C"]`; `notation-dark` `["#606060", "#858585", "#ACACAC", "#D5D5D5", "#FFFFFF"]`.
