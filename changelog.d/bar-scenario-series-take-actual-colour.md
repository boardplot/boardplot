### Changed / may need action

- Bar: a series with `scenario: "PY"`, `"PL"` or `"FC"` now takes the colour of the actual it is compared with, as columns do. Both chart types share one pairing rule. The k-th PY, PL or FC series pairs with the k-th actual series (`scenario: "AC"` or no scenario), in authored order, and takes no colour step of its own. A series whose cells all carry one scenario counts as that scenario. PY draws the actual's colour as the 45 % wash toward the page, FC hatches it and PL outlines it. Series order no longer changes the colours. A PY cell in a series of several scenarios draws the same wash. In a stack only a plan pairs: its outline takes the colour of the k-th plain segment. A series with its own `fill` or `colorRole` keeps it.
- Before, each bar series kept the colour of its place in the series list, and a PY bar drew the full series colour. With PY, AC, PL and FC in that order, PY took the theme's first series colour, AC the second, PL the third and FC the fourth. In `notation` the prior year then printed darker than the actual. To bring back the old look, give each series its old colour as `fill`. For that order: `boardroom` `#12395E`, `#5E99C5`, `#B58629`, `#72767C`; `standard` `#075A8E`, `#55BEF7`, `#2AB1A9`, `#838CDA`; `standard-dark` `#087397`, `#55BEFB`, `#25AAA2`, `#828BD9`; `notation` `#6D6D6D`, `#2A2A2A`, `#939393`, `#4A4A4A`; `notation-dark` `#606060`, `#858585`, `#ACACAC`, `#D5D5D5`; `capital` `#2E346F`, `#C48404`, `#1F7761`, `#905111`; `studio` `#24564E`, `#C68610`, `#C1553F`, `#609E93`; `glass` `#4E8BE6`, `#35CDB3`, `#C6A4FF`, `#B5F5C8`. For another order, the n-th series took the theme's n-th colour (`theme.color.series`). A stacked plan took its step of the stack ramp.
