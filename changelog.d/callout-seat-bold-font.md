### Fixed

- A callout on a variance, slope or waterfall chart no longer runs past its seat onto the label beside it. These charts draw callout text bold, but the seat was measured in the regular weight, so a long callout covered a neighbouring value and could hide its minus sign. The seat is now measured bold. The drawing keeps its bold weight.
- A callout no longer seats on top of the 45° category names of a column, line, waterfall, variance or heatmap chart. A rotated name claimed only a small square at its anchor, so a callout could take the strip under the axis where the name runs. The name now also claims the slanted box its text covers. A callout that has no other free seat is left out of the drawing and printed in the notes under the chart, as for any callout without a seat.
