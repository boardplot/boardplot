### Fixed

- A callout on a variance, slope or waterfall chart no longer runs past its seat onto the label beside it. These charts draw callout text bold, but the seat was measured in the regular weight, so a long callout covered a neighbouring value and could hide its minus sign. The seat is now measured bold, for the callouts that draw bold. An arrow callout and the body under a headline still draw regular, and their seat is measured regular. The drawing keeps its weights.
- A callout no longer seats on top of the 45° category names of a column, line, waterfall, variance or heatmap chart. A rotated name claimed only a small square at its anchor, so a callout could take the strip under the axis where the name runs. The name now also claims the slanted box its text covers. A callout that has no other free seat is left out of the drawing and printed in the notes under the chart, as for any callout without a seat.
- A tall chart no longer loses its rotated category names. On a narrow canvas a taller plot let the 45° names grow until the row had no pitch left for a rotated line, and all but one name were dropped. Each rotated name is now capped at the length its own seat allows, so the row keeps its pitch. Only a name longer than its own cap is cut and noted, as before; a long name elsewhere in the row no longer shortens the others.
