### Changed / may need action

- Column, combo (pareto included) and variance: a row of category names now steps down to the reader floor size of its target and wraps on up to three lines before it rotates. Before, the row stepped down by 1 px (column, combo) or not at all (variance) and then turned its names 45° (or -90° on a pareto at a fixed canvas height) and cut them to a few letters when one word was wider than its slot. On a full slide the floor is 12 pt over a 13 or 14 pt axis. A name with a hyphen may break after the hyphen, where it would otherwise be cut. Names that fit at the axis size keep it, and a row that still does not fit at the floor size rotates as before, at the sizes it used before. Rows of dates and numbered sequences are unchanged. There is no option for the old look.
