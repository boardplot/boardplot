### Added

- A metric stack takes `options.stack.variant: 'focus'`, a value-first look with the compare window as the focus. Each panel's readout prints the value of the focus large, then the change and the base value: `$46.4` over `−18% from $56.5`. A stacked panel keys each measure with its share of the focus value, in whole percent that add up to 100 (`Monthly plan 56%`), under an optional caption, `keyTitle: 'By first order'`, which also prints in the default look. The header line reads `Week of 21 Sep 2026 against ┄ week of 2 Mar`. Two theme roles size the values: `type.displayLarge` (72 px) for the panel with `emphasis`, `type.displaySmall` (36 px) for the others, in the family and weight of `type.figure`. `strings.stackFrom`, `strings.stackAgainst` and `strings.stackAverages` replace the new words. A stack without the option draws as before.
