### Changed

- A sankey (its node and link values) and a butterfly or a pyramid (both sides together, so a row reads at one magnitude) print their values at one magnitude, as the funnel does: the largest step at which the smallest value still prints as 1 or more, while the largest keeps at most four digits. So `1,505M · 935M` instead of `1.505B · 935M`. A butterfly side with its own unit or format is its own set. A set that no single magnitude fits keeps one scale per label as before. A value that prints 1,000 or more at the set's magnitude prints whole. Decimals and shares print as before. No option or theme patch brings back the old look for every chart. A chart `format` holds every label to the scale of its example, for example `"format": "1.2B"`.
