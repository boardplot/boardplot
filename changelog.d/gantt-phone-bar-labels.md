### Fixed

- A gantt on a phone no longer prints a bar name over a bar it is wider than. When an event or today rule blocks the seat beside the bar, the name takes that seat and the rule breaks around it. A name above or below a bar in a row with milestone flags no longer slides away from its bar, because a flag's leader now blocks only its own side of the row. When the week or month cells are too narrow to hold a name, the calendar head prints the name on every second or third cell instead of leaving the row empty.
