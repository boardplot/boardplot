### Fixed

- A chart that moves a printed share so a whole adds up to 100% now says so in its notes list. A column, bar, combo, bar table cascade or stacked 100% area chart defaults to `rounding: "sum-consistent"`, and three equal thirds printed 34%, 33% and 33% with only `reading.rounding` showing the move. The chart gets one method note, which never prints under the chart, also where `notes.show` lists `"method"`, so the drawing does not change. The note never costs a chart its draw or its size: a pinned dashboard card draws at the same size, and when the note is the only hidden note and React's notes button would need a row of its own, the button is left off. The area note follows the drawn window. On a column or bar stack with `sharesOfOneWhole`, the note also names a stack whose authored parts miss 100 by more than 0.05, because each share is its part divided by the stack total. The manual now names every type that takes `options.rounding` and its default.
