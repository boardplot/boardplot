### Fixed

- An average, median or anomaly bound under a scaled format keeps the decimals it needs. On a column, bar, line or combo chart with format `1M`, an average of 1.25M printed as `1M`, because its decimals were counted on 1,250,000 and not on 1.25. The label now counts them at the printed scale and shows `1.3M`, and `1.25M` under a format with two decimals.
- A mean `valueLine` prints the true average on every chart that draws one. On a line, column or dot chart, an average of 1.25M under format `1M` printed `1M` and under `1.0M` printed `1.3M`, and an average of 2,500 under `$1k` printed `$3k`, while a bar printed `1.3M`, `1.25M` and `$2.5k`. All charts now print the bar's text, and a dot chart's `averageMarker` does too. A mean under an authored format that rounds it, such as `0.00` on a variance chart, shows one decimal more (`5.333` for 5.3333), as on a bar. An authored `format` on the line itself still wins, and gives back the old text.
