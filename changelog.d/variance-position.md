### Added

- Grid option `variancePosition` for category tiers: `"above"` (default) stands the variance tiers over the column panel, with the category labels at the bottom of the exhibit; `"below"` stands them under it in authored order, with the category labels between the columns and the tiers. The default is unchanged. Before, a variance tier listed first to stand under the columns took the category labels and grew the grid to several times its height.
