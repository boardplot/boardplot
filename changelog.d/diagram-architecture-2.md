### Added

- The diagram `architecture` variant gains zones and marks for trust and data boundaries. A group's `parentId` nests zones up to three levels, and a group or node with `protected: true` draws a double frame and a lock. This is an authored claim. A group with `kind: 'policy'` becomes a strip across the exhibit, and its nodes take no edges or `laneId`. A node can use the new `store` shape, and `repeat` (2 to 5) draws stacked copies. An edge end can name a zone with `{ groupId }`, and it then stops on the frame. An edge can start and end on one node. `data.callouts` puts one numbered disc per node, edge or zone, each explained in a key row. In `lanes` flow, a zone must contain a node to carry a callout. `options.highlight` names node and edge ids, and the rest of the exhibit recedes. Channel edges and edges that cross a protected frame draw an open marker on the frame. A cycle in `parentId` is refused with `diagram-group-cycle`. `lanes` flow refuses nested zones.
