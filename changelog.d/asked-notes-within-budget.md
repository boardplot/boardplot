### Changed

- A disclosure asked for only by `methodNote: true` or an authored `notes.placement` now prints within the line budget, after the author notes, like a method note. What does not fit goes to the notes list, and "+N notes" counts it. Before, such a disclosure printed outside the budget in a live chart, so a React table with `notes.placement: "frame"` and five author notes printed two of them, three engine notes and "+3 notes". Outside the budget print only a required credit, an author note a mark points at, and a disclosure whose kind the author lists in `notes.show`. The option patch `{ "notes": { "show": ["note", "method", "disclosure"] } }` brings back the old footer. Static SVG and PNG output does not change: it prints every enabled note. A chart that hands its options to another chart type (cohort, likert, pareto, pyramid, pvm) treats `methodNote: true` the same way.
