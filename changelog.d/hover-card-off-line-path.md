### Fixed

- On a line, area, combo or sparkline chart in React, the hover card no longer stops the pointer on its way along the line. Before, the card often opened over the line beside its point and held that point while the pointer moved along the line toward it. On a 176 × 42 px sparkline, the card of point 22 of 24 opened over the line to its left, and a pointer moving left could not reach most of the earlier points. Now a card that can stand clear of the area between the points, within a short reach of its point, stands there. On the small sparkline it opens above the chart, and the pointer can still move onto it to read it. On a chart with several lines, the card stays beside its point, over the other lines. A card over the lines no longer stays open under the pointer: the pointer passes through it and picks the point under it. A card from `renderHoverContent` always keeps clear of the lines and still takes the pointer.
