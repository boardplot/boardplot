### Added

- A `cagr` annotation takes `shape: "arrow"`: one straight arrow over the marks with the rate in an oval on it. The default stays the bracket. The oval prints the rate alone; `period: true` adds the years. Where no straight line clears the chart, the annotation draws the bracket and warns `cagr-arrow-no-room`. The fixture `stacked-revenue-cagr-arrow` shows the form.
