### Changed / may need action

- A KPI card, a pie, a choropleth and a column now draw on a canvas narrower than 280 px, down to 120 px (160 px for a map), when their content fits. Five or six KPI tiles in a dashboard row, a 240 px pie and a four-bar column in a 200 px tile no longer get `size-below-floor`. The floor is the measured content: a KPI's figure, a pie's circle (radius at least 36 px) with its labels, a map's key and small members, and a column's bars (at least 3 px wide). A canvas at 280 px and wider draws exactly as before. `measure` and `data-bp-sizing` now report the lower `minWidth` of a KPI card, a pie and a map, and a compact KPI card has the same floor as a regular one. The `compactFloor` that `measure` verifies for a column and a pie stays 280 px. A host that needs the old floor passes `belowMinimum: 'refuse'` or its own size.
