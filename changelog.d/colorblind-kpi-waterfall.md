### Changed / may need action

- `colorblindSafe` is accepted on every chart type, so one setting can cover a whole deck. A chart with no good/bad pair draws the same as without it, including a table without tones and a map with a sequential scale, which were refused before. KPI deltas, chips, attainment and bands, and waterfall and pvm steps, signed labels and key swatches, now use the theme's colour-blind pair.
