### Changed / may need action

- A value heatmap and a cohort heatmap now shorten the number text before they hide a number. Before, a cell that had no room for "117.80" or "72%" lost its number and kept only its colour, so a half-slide or card matrix often printed no number at all. Now, when a number does not fit at the label floor, the whole matrix prints one shorter text. A % sign that the unit line names goes first (a cohort rate prints "72", not "72%"), then one decimal at a time, down to the fewest decimals at which the values still fall on ten steps across their range. The first text at which every number fits wins, and a column that holds one value in every cell (a cohort's first period) may stay blank. When no text fits, the full text stays and the numbers without room are hidden as before. A matrix that fits keeps its text, and so do a format and a series format you set yourself. The hover card and the reading keep every decimal and the sign. The colour key follows the cells and keeps the decimals a tick needs, so a tick at 2.5 never reads "3".
- To bring back the old text, set `options.format`: `'1,234.00'` (or the decimals you want) on a heatmap keeps its two decimals, and `'1%'` on a cohort heatmap keeps the % sign. The old look hides the numbers that miss their cell.
