### Changed

- The title and caption of a stacked panel now stand clearly apart from the panel above and close to their own chart. Before, a grid with `sharedAxis: 'category'` put 5 px (4 px in `notation`) between the upper panel and the heading under it, the same space as between the heading and its own legend or plot, so the heading read like a note of the chart above. The space above a panel heading is now 20 px in most presets (16 px in `notation`): the tier gap plus three times `space.labelPad`. A grid with an open height grows by that room, and a fixed height that has no room for it keeps the old gap. The new theme token `space.tierHeadingGap` sets this space. The patch `{ "space": { "tierHeadingGap": 5 } }` brings back the old look (`4` for `notation` and `notation-dark`). Panels without a heading and `options.stack` keep their spacing.
