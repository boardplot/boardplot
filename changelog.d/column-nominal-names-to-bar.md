### Fixed

- A column chart whose category names neither fit under the columns nor rotate now draws as horizontal bars, which have room for every name. Before, it drew a few names and listed the rest as `redundant`, so nothing showed that names were lost. The step needs an open height, or a pinned height that holds every row, and no setting that only a column has (`anomaly`, `ratioTier`, `events`, `seriesRamp`, `othersShade`, `seriesLabels`, `shareLabels`, `window`, `viewDomain`, `categoryLabels`, `ribbons`, the value axis keys `tickStep` and `percent`, a category with `role: 'total'`, or a stacked PY reference series that draws growth labels). The info warning `column-stepped-to-bar` names the step. Its records and hover read the bars. A column that stays reports each dropped name of a nominal row as `density`, not `redundant`. Dated rows still thin their labels as before. To keep the column, pin a height that cannot hold one row per name (`size.height`), or set one of the column-only settings above. For a stepped chart, `measure` gives the natural and comfortable sizes of the chart drawn at its natural width. No other setting restores the old look. The `reading` of a stepped chart reports `type` as `bar`.
