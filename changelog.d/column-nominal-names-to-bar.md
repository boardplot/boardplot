### Fixed

- A column chart whose category names neither fit under the columns nor rotate now draws as horizontal bars, which have room for every name. Before, it drew a few names and listed the rest as `redundant`, so nothing showed that names were lost. The step needs an open height, or a pinned height that holds every row. It is made only when every setting and data key of the request is one a bar draws as a column does: the frame and text options (`target`, `textScale`, `textSize`, `a11y`, `mode`, `margin`, `canvas`, `title`, `subtitle`, `unit`, `unitShown`, `source`, `footnotes`, `notes`, `theme`, `locale`, `format`), `variant` `grouped` or `stacked`, `sort`, `highlight`, `focus`, `signColor`, `legend`, `valueLabels`, `labelDensity`, `netLine: true`, `averageMarker`, `labels.restSeries`, the value axis keys `show`, `min` and `includeZero`, the wording keys `net`, `average`, `others`, `toDate`, `hiddenLabels`, `valuesNotOnColumns`, `aboveZero`, `belowZero`, `knownPartsTotals` and `moreListed`, the annotations `note`, `callout`, `diff`, `delta`, `band`, one `cagr` and a `valueLine` without `seriesId`, `from` or `to`, categories with only `id`, `label`, `footnote` and `partial`, and plain cells (a value, a gap, `fill`, `format`, `label`, `pct`, `scenario`) in series without `role`. Any other setting keeps the column, so a setting added to the column later stays on the column until it is checked against the bar. The info warning `column-stepped-to-bar` names the step. Its records and hover read the bars. A column that stays reports each dropped name of a nominal row as `density`, not `redundant`. A column refused at a pinned width below its floor keeps the refusal it had before. Dated rows and numbered sequences such as D0 to D33 still thin their labels at a calm stride as before, and list the names left out as `redundant`. To keep the column, pin a height that cannot hold one row per name (`size.height`), or set a setting outside the list above. For a stepped chart, `measure` gives the natural and comfortable sizes of the chart drawn at its natural width. The `reading` of a stepped chart reports `type` as `bar`.
