### Fixed

- A reference line stays visible where it crosses an area of its own colour. The Bridge palette paints the top stacked series in the ink colour, and a dashed value line across it disappeared, so "Benchmark 100" pointed at nothing. A value line, axis line or leader over an area, a fan band or the fill between two lines now turns light over a dark fill, as it already did over a bar. Era shade edges keep their page colour. Changed goldens: `line/stacked-area-benchmark` and `line/fan-torture` (the zero line over the darkest band), both variants. `line/gap-bracket` changes bytes only and draws the same pixels.
- A map overlay marker stays visible on a region of its own colour. A solid marker disc, square or diamond, a full Harvey ball and a one-share pie now have a page-colour ring, as status discs already had. Before, the Information marker on the darkest Bridge class (navy on navy, and near-black on near-black in `notation`) disappeared. Changed goldens: `choropleth/info-glyphs-with-status`, `choropleth/world-footprint-glyphs` and `tilemap/us-states-overlay-glyphs`, both variants.
