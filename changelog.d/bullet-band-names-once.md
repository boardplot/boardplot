### Fixed

- A bullet chart that prints its band names on the rows no longer names the bands a second time in the key with the built-in words Worse, Middle and Better. The key now holds Actual and Target. It adds the ramp only when the author sets the words in `strings.bandKey`. To bring back the old key, set the words for the band count of the chart in `strings`: `{"strings":{"bandKey":["Worse","Better"]}}` for two bands, `["Worse","Middle","Better"]` for three, `["Worse","Lower middle","Upper middle","Better"]` for four and `["Worse","Lower middle","Middle","Upper middle","Better"]` for five.
