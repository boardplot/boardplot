### Fixed

- Column, combo and grid no longer add a magnitude letter on top of a unit that names a scale. Before, values of a million or more under "in $m" printed as "105.0M", "20M" or "12M": the compact stack totals of a narrow column card, the panels of a grid whose own unit sat on the grid, and a combo secondary line without a unit of its own. They now print whole and grouped ("105,000,000"), as the column labels, the bar table and the combo primary axis already did, and the engine still warns `unit-scale-digits`. A grid's scale unit now reaches every panel that names no unit in its options or its data; such a panel follows that unit's number rule and leaves the printing of the unit to the grid, and a kpi panel no longer adds it to its label. A combo series that names no unit beside a series that names "$m" now reads in "$m", as the unit check already read it: "24M" prints as "24,000,000". To print a letter as before, give the series or the panel a format such as `"format": "1.2M"`. This changes the drawing of such panels in every respect a standalone chart under that unit already showed: a column panel with 105.3 and 98.7 under the grid's "$m" printed "105.3 | 98.7" and now prints "105 | 98.7", as a standalone column with unit "$m" does, because the unit rule prints values from 100 up without decimals. A dot panel and a scatter x axis under the grid's "$m" printed "1.2M" and "7.2M" and now print "1,200,000" and "7,200,000".
