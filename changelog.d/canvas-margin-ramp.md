### Changed

- The default canvas margin no longer narrows the plot as the canvas widens. At each margin step the side margins now start at the margins under the step and grow by one pixel per pixel of width until they reach the step's margins: from 600 to 615 px they grow from 16 to 24 px, from 840 to 847 px from 24 to 28 px, and from 1080 to 1087 px from 28 to 32 px. Widths under a step keep their margins. A chart that draws at a width now draws at every wider width. Before, a bar table that drew at 835 to 839 px was refused at 840 to 842 px. It now draws at every width from 835 px.
- To bring back the old look: no theme key can express the old stepped margins, because `space.canvas.base` sets one margin for every width under 1080 px and `space.canvas.wide` one for every width from 1080 px. At one width, pass the old sides as the `margin` option: `{ "left": 24, "right": 24 }` from 600 to 615 px, `{ "left": 28, "right": 28 }` from 840 to 847 px, and `{ "left": 32, "right": 32 }` from 1080 to 1087 px. A theme that sets `space.canvas.base` or `space.canvas.wide` keeps its own margins.
