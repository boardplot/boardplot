### Fixed

- An integrated variance chart no longer drops a large variance while smaller ones keep their values. A variance in a valley between taller columns had both seats over its cap blocked by the neighbouring columns and their values, so it went to the notes list. On a 24-month chart at 480 px, +7.9, the largest variance, had no label while +1.0 and +0.1 kept theirs. The chart draws as many values as before the fix found seats for. When one of that many largest variances has no seat, it now rises clear of the neighbouring columns and their values, on a leader down to its own cap (below them for a variance under the baseline). The values already drawn do not move, and a smaller variance never takes such a seat. Before, these values were only listed in the notes. There is no option to bring the old look back: it only ever hid labels.
