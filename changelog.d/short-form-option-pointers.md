### Fixed

- A finding about an option written in short form points at the option the author wrote. Before, `sort: "desc"` was read as `{ categories: "desc" }`, so a table refusal returned `/options/sort/categories` and `received: missing`, and a cohort warning proposed the patch `remove /options/sort/categories`, which cannot apply to a string. Both now name `/options/sort`, also inside a grid panel. A patch that adds a field inside a short form replaces the option with its expanded form: the butterfly `butterfly-sort-ignored` warning proposes `replace /options/sort {"categories": "sheet"}`. The short forms are `sort` (`true` or a direction), `topN` (a number), `locale` (a language tag), `footnotes` (one string) and `source` (a list of strings).
