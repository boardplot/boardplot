### Changed

- Beside the Target and Gap-to-target columns of a bullet chart, the name and the numbers of a row now stand at the height of its bar. Before, a two-line name or gap pushed the target and the gap up to the top line of the row, above the actual value on the same bar. No setting brings back the old placement.
- The line under each row name (higher or lower is better) and the line under each gap (below, above or on target) no longer print by default, because the sign and the colour of the gap already say both. Without them, some charts are lower, and some narrower charts now fit the Target and Gap columns. `"labels": { "direction": true, "position": true }` brings both lines back. A line that is switched on hangs below its text, and each row takes about 15 px more height: the `ops-dashboard` example at 720 px wide is 608 px tall with a line and 488 px without.
- A row without a target prints `n/a` in its Target and Gap cells, as a bar chart prints a value it cannot show, and no direction line under any setting. Before, the cells stayed empty and the name printed "higher is better". `strings.notApplicable` sets the glyph, and `"strings": { "notApplicable": "" }` leaves the cells empty again.
- Beside the Target and Gap-to-target columns, the Actual column now stands a little further from the bars: 12 px after the end of the widest band or bar instead of 6 px.
