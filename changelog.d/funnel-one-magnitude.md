### Changed

- A funnel prints its segment values and stage totals at one magnitude: the largest step at which the smallest value still prints as 1 or more, while the largest keeps at most four digits. So `1,180M · 590M · 290M · 140M` instead of `1.18B · 590M · 290M · 140M`, and `1,200k · 300k · 45k · 8k` instead of `1.2M · 300k · 45k · 8k`. A set that no single magnitude fits, such as `2.4M · 86.0k · 4.1k · 310.0`, keeps one scale per label as before, and so does a funnel with an authored format on the chart, a series or a cell. Decimals and conversion rates print as before. No option or theme patch brings back the old look for every chart. A cell format per value restores the old text exactly, for example `"values": [{ "number": 1180000000, "format": "1.23B" }, { "number": 590000000, "format": "1M" }]`.
