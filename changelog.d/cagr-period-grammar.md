### Fixed

- A CAGR read from category labels now counts the years from the whole period, not from the last word. `Jan 2023` to `Jul 2024` is 1.5 years, so 100 to 160 prints +36.8% p.a., not +60.0%. The same holds for `January 2023`, `Jan '23`, `Mär 2023`, `Q1 FY23`, `1H 2023` and `Revenue Q4 2023`, on a CAGR annotation, in `seriesLabels.cagr` and in the bar table cascade growth column. A two-digit start year after the end moves to the century before, so `FY98` to `FY02` is 4 years. Labels with different words before the period, such as `Revenue Q4 2023` and `Cost Q1 2025`, week labels and labels the parser cannot read print no rate. A CAGR annotation warns `cagr-no-dates` for them, a bar table cascade refuses with `bartable-cascade-growth-years`, and `seriesLabels` warns `series-cagr-no-span`. Different leading names still count the years when they are no part of the period and both periods sit at the same point in the year, such as `Plan 2024` and `Actual 2026F`, `FY 2023` and `FY2024`, or `Actual Q4 2023` and `Plan Q4 2025`.
- A CAGR between ISO dates in category ids now prints its rate. The same month and day on both dates is a whole number of years, so 28 February 2023 to 28 February 2024 is 1 year and no longer warns `cagr-short-span`.
